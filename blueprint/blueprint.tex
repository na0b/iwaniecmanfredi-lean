\title[Blueprint of the Lean formalization]{Regularity of $p$-harmonic functions on the plane: blueprint of the Lean formalization}
\author{Tadeusz Iwaniec}
\author{Juan J. Manfredi}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
This document collects the natural-language statements and detailed proofs that accompany the Lean~4 formalization of the theorems, corollaries and lemmas of T.~Iwaniec and J.~J.~Manfredi, \emph{Regularity of $p$-harmonic functions on the plane}, Rev.\ Mat.\ Iberoamericana \textbf{5} (1989), 1--19.
Three groups of results that the article quotes from the literature (regularity of the complex gradient of a $p$-harmonic function; the basic theory of quasiregular and quasiconformal mappings; smoothness off the zero set) are not proved; they are stated precisely and carried as explicit hypotheses (\texttt{CGradPackage}, \texttt{QCPackage}, \texttt{SmoothOffZeros}). Everything else is proved.
Each subsection corresponds to the Lean declaration of the same name in the directory \texttt{IwaniecManfredi/}; cross-references point to the results that a proof uses.
Section~\ref{sec:main} contains the formalized results of the article.
Within each section, items appear in dependency order.
\end{abstract}
