\begin{definition}
Let $H\colon\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$ and let $d=(i,j,\beta)$ be a derivative
symbol in the sense of \noderef{DerivSymbol}.  The \emph{value of $d$ on $H$}
is the function $H^d\colon\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$ given by
\[
  H^d=\begin{cases}
    \dfrac{\partial^{i+j}H}{\partial\xi^i\partial\bar\xi^j}
      &\text{if }\beta\text{ is false},\\[2.5ex]
    \overline{\dfrac{\partial^{i+j}H}{\partial\xi^i\partial\bar\xi^j}}
      &\text{if }\beta\text{ is true},
  \end{cases}
\]
where $\partial^{i+j}H/\partial\xi^i\partial\bar\xi^j$ is the iterated
Wirtinger derivative of \noderef{WirtingerIter} and the bar denotes complex
conjugation of the value.

Thus $H^d$ is precisely one of the partials denoted $H^s$ in Section~5 of the
paper, with $s=i+j=|d|$.
\end{definition}
