\begin{definition}
Let $\Omega\subset\mathbb{C}$.  A function $\phi\colon\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$
is a \emph{test function for $\Omega$} if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\phi$ is $C^\infty$-smooth as a function of the two real variables
$(x,y)$ on all of $\mathbb{C}$,
\item[(b)] the support of $\phi$ is compact, and
\item[(c)] that support is contained in $\Omega$.
\end{enumerate}
Here the support is the closure of $\{z:\phi(z)\neq0\}$.  This is the class
$C^\infty_c(\Omega)$ of smooth functions with compact support in $\Omega$,
used below to express distributional derivatives and the weak form of the
$p$-harmonic equation (1).
\end{definition}
