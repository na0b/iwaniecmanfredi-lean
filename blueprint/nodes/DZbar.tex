\begin{definition}
With the identification $z=x+iy$ of $\mathbb{C}$ with $\mathbb{R}^2$ and the
real Fr\'echet derivative $Df(z)$ of a map $f$ at $z$ (a real-linear map
$\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$, so that $Df(z)[1]=f_x(z)$ and $Df(z)[i]=f_y(z)$),
the second operator of
complex differentiation
\[
  \frac{\partial}{\partial\bar z}=\frac12\Bigl(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+i\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\Bigr)
\]
of Section~2 of the paper is given, for $f\colon\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$, by
\[
  \frac{\partial f}{\partial\bar z}(z)=\frac12\bigl(Df(z)[1]+i\,Df(z)[i]\bigr).
\]
We write $f_{\bar z}$ for this function of $z$, and $H_{\bar\xi}$ when the
variable is called $\xi$.

At a point where $f$ is not real-differentiable, $Df(z)$ is
taken to be the zero map, so the expression is defined at every point of
$\mathbb{C}$.
\end{definition}
