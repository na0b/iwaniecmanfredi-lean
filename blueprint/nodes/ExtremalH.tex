\begin{definition}
Let $1<p<\infty$, let $d=d(p)$ be the exponent of \noderef{DExp} and let
$\epsilon=\epsilon_2(1,p)$ be the coefficient of \noderef{EpsCoef} taken with
$n=1$ and $k=2$.  Define $H\colon\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$ by
\[
  H(\xi)=\Bigl(\frac{\xi}{|\xi|}+\epsilon\,\frac{|\xi|^3}{\xi^3}\Bigr)\,|\xi|^{1/d}.
\]

This is the extremal function (54) of Section~7 of the paper, which Section~7
obtains from the series (19) by taking $n=1$ in the factorization (15) and
$A_1=0$, $A_2=1$, $A_3=A_4=\dots=0$ in (19); there
$\lambda_2=\gamma_1=1/d$ and $\epsilon=\epsilon_2=(1-d)/(1+3d)$, which is the
number $\epsilon_2(1,p)$ used here.

At $\xi=0$ both quotients are read as $0$, under the convention $w/0=0$, so the
first factor vanishes and the formula gives $H(0)=0$; this is the value the
paper's $H$ takes at the origin, and it is what makes $H$ continuous there,
since $1/d>0$.
\end{definition}
