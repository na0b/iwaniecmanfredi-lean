\begin{definition}
For a map $H\colon\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$ put
\[
  J(\xi)=|H_\xi(\xi)|^2-|H_{\bar\xi}(\xi)|^2,
\]
with $H_\xi$ as in \noderef{DZ} and $H_{\bar\xi}$ as in \noderef{DZbar}.  This
is the Jacobian determinant of $H$, viewed as a map of two real variables; it
is the quantity $J(\xi)$ appearing in equation (17) of the paper and again in
equation (42).
\end{definition}
