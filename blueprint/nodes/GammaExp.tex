\begin{definition}
For an integer $n\ge0$ and a real number $p$ put
\[
  \gamma_n=\gamma_n(p)=\lambda_{n+1}(n,p),
\]
with $\lambda_k(n,p)$ as in \noderef{LambdaCoef}.  This is the exponent
$\gamma_n$ of equation (44) of the paper.

Equation (44) displays $\gamma_n$ in the expanded form
\[
  \gamma_n=\frac n2\Bigl\{-p+\sqrt{4\bigl(1+\tfrac1n\bigr)^2(p-1)+(p-2)^2}\Bigr\},
\]
which for $n\ge1$ is the same number, because
$4(n+1)^2(p-1)+n^2(p-2)^2=n^2\bigl(4(1+\frac1n)^2(p-1)+(p-2)^2\bigr)$ and
$n>0$.
\end{definition}
