\begin{definition}
Let $\Omega\subset\mathbb{C}$, let $1\le s\le\infty$ and let
$u\colon\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$.  We say that $u$ belongs to
$L^s_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)$ if for every compact $K\subset\Omega$ the
function $\mathbf{1}_K\,u$, equal to $u$ on $K$ and to $0$ off $K$, lies in
$L^s(\mathbb{C})$ with respect to two-dimensional Lebesgue measure; that is,
$\mathbf{1}_K u$ is measurable and
\[
  \int_{K}|u|^s\,d\sigma<\infty\qquad(s<\infty),
  \qquad
  \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{K}|u|<\infty\qquad(s=\infty).
\]
This is the space $L^s_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)$ used in Section~1 of the paper.
\end{definition}
