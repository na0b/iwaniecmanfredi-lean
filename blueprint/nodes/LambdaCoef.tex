\begin{definition}
For integers $n\ge0$ and $k\ge0$ and a real number $p$ put
\[
  \lambda_k(n,p)=\frac12\Bigl(-np+\sqrt{4k^2(p-1)+n^2(p-2)^2}\Bigr).
\]
This is the quantity $\lambda_k=\lambda_k(n,p)$ of equation (20) of the paper
(equation (37) repeats the same formula).

Here $\sqrt{\,\cdot\,}$ denotes the real square root, extended by $\sqrt t=0$
for $t<0$, so that the formula defines a real number for every real $p$.  The
paper uses $\lambda_k(n,p)$ only in the range $1<p<\infty$, $k\ge n\ge1$,
where the radicand $4k^2(p-1)+n^2(p-2)^2$ is a sum of two nonnegative terms
and the extension is never invoked.
\end{definition}
